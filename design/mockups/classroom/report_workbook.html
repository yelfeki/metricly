Concept reminders · week 12
Job Demands–Resources
Demands cost effort and cause strain; resources buffer demands and fuel motivation.
Demand–Control (Karasek)
Strain is worst when demands are high and control is low.
Appraisal & coping
Stress is the gap you perceive between demands and your ability to cope.
Recovery
Detachment and rest replenish drained resources.
